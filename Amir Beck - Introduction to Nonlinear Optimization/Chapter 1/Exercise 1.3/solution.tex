$\mathbf{1.3.}$ \textbf{Show that for any} $\mathbf{x,y,z \in \mathbb{R}^n}$
\begin{align*}
    \mathbf{||x-z|| \leq ||x-y||+||y-z||.}
\end{align*}

\vspace{0.3cm}

Let $\mathbf{x,y,z} \in \mathbb{R}^n$, then
\begin{align*}
    ||\mathbf{x-z||} &= ||\mathbf{(x-y)+(y-z)}|| \leq ||\mathbf{x-y}||+||\mathbf{y-z}||,
\end{align*}

where in the last step we used the triangle inequality property of the norm.
