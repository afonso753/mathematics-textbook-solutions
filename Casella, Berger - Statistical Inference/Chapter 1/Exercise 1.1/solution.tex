$\mathbf{1.1}$ For each of the following experiments, describe the sample space.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Toss a coin four times.
    \item Count the number of insect-damaged leaves on a plant.
    \item Measure the lifetime (in hours) of a particular brand of light bulb.
    \item Record the weights of a 10-day-old rats.
    \item Observe the proportion of defectives in a shipment of electronic components.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item $\Omega = \{H,T\}^4 = \{HHHH, HHHT, HHTH, HTHH, THHH, HHTT,\\ HTHT, HTTH, THHT, THTH,TTHH, HTTT,THTT,TTHT,TTTH,\\TTTT\}$, where $H$ and $T$ are the events of getting Heads and Tails, respectively, when tossing a coin and $ABCD$ represents the event of getting $A$ on the first toss, $B$ on the second toss, $C$ on the third toss and $D$ on the fourth toss, where $A,B,C,D \in \{H,T\}$.
    \item $\Omega = \{0,1,2,...,n\}$, where $n$ is the total number of leaves on the plant.
    \item $\Omega = [0,\infty)$.
    \item $\Omega = (0,\infty)^n$, where $n$ is the number of 10-day-old rats involved in the experiment.
    \item $\Omega=\left\{0, \frac{1}{n}, \frac{2}{n}, ..., \frac{n-1}{n}, 1 \right\}$, where $n$ is the number of electronic components on the shipment.
\end{enumerate}
